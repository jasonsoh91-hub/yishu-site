# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Malaysian consumers evaluating Yishu skincare products, and prospective Malaysian partners evaluating the Yishu marketing plan.

## Product Purpose

Present Yishu's company, product technology, product range, certificates, people, consultant journey, and partnership model for the Malaysian market, then turn interest into a direct enquiry.

## Positioning

Yishu combines a smart electroporation face mask, biotechnology-led skincare, documented standards, and a partner-led local sales model.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a long-form multilingual marketing page, compare the smart mask with familiar care routines, review certificates and product sets, and submit an enquiry for a product, consultation, or partnership.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- English, Simplified Chinese, and Bahasa Melayu routes.
- The public `https://yishu.su/en/` site is the binding visual and content reference.
- The Malaysian implementation should reproduce the reference site's visual design, imagery, section order, people, claims, and interactions as closely as the web stack permits.
- Ruble-denominated procedure and income figures are localized to Malaysian ringgit (RM).
- Brand level thresholds remain denominated in US dollars.
- Russian phone and email details must not appear. Malaysian contact details remain an open owner decision and must not be fabricated.
- The lead form delivers to a Google Sheet via `app/api/lead/route.ts` (see README).
- The reseller holds a KPDN direct-sales licence (confirmed 2026-10-27); add the licence number to the footer when available.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the Yishu and 宜树 names.
- Preserve the live reference site's established dark forest, cream, gold, editorial typography, imagery, motion, and responsive behavior.
- Localization must not turn the page into a visually different regional redesign.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live reference: `https://yishu.su/en/`.
- Public imagery, certificates, and page markup are available from the live reference.
- The local Next.js implementation and multilingual content are already present.
- Malaysian reseller phone, email, address, social links, domain, and confirmed local procedure prices have not yet been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Match the established Yishu experience before adding regional variation.
- Localize commercial details without weakening brand recognition.
- Keep factual claims and evidence traceable to the reference site.
- Never fabricate Malaysian business, contact, registration, or pricing details.
- Make the enquiry path clear and usable on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, semantic structure, and responsive behavior while reproducing the reference design.
